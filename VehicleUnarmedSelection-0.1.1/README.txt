UNARMED IN VEHICLES — GTA IV 1.0.8.0
Version 0.1.1

Adds fists/unarmed to the vehicle weapon cycle so you can put your gun away
while driving. Use your normal vehicle weapon-cycle controls. No new hotkey.

REQUIREMENTS
- GTA IV 1.0.8.0
- An ASI loader.

INSTALLATION
Close the game. Copy VehicleUnarmedSelection.asi and
VehicleUnarmedSelection.ini next to GTAIV.exe. Install one copy only.
In the source package, building creates these files under bin.

DISABLE / UNINSTALL
Set Enabled=0 under [MAIN] in the INI and restart to disable.
To uninstall, close the game and remove this ASI, INI and its log.
Do not hot-load or unload the ASI during gameplay.

COMPATIBILITY
Intended for singleplayer. The mod changes weapon selection and leaves
inventory, ammunition, weapon definitions, and aim/fire controls untouched.
It guards against automatic re-equipping while unarmed; explicit scripted
weapon changes can still equip a weapon.
